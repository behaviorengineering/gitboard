gitboard serves as a local code-change dashboard for GitLab and GitHub. It provides a unified interface to monitor CI status, track open Merge Requests and Pull Requests, and perform failed job triage. By integrating with `gh`, `glab`, and `git` on the local machine, it synchronizes remote forge activity with local checkout and worktree states to streamline developer workflows.
