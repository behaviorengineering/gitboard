Gitboard provides a local, high-visibility dashboard for developers to monitor their work across multiple Git forges (GitHub and GitLab). 

The system centralizes CI status, open Merge Requests/Pull Requests, and failed job triage into a single local interface. By leveraging existing CLI tools (`gh`, `glab`, `git`) and optional LLM-based triage, Gitboard helps developers maintain situational awareness of their projects and local worktree states without leaving their terminal or local environment.
