# Architecture
> Teaching story. Living project architecture for humans and review grounding. Not Typology seed evidence (see `evidence/typology/architecture_brief.md`).
The Majordomo architecture evolves as a structured orchestration of capabilities, moving from raw discovery toward a refined state of managed interactions. Rather than a static blueprint, the system shape emerges through the continuous alignment of package roles, capability constraints, and objective ledgers.
The system organizes around discrete functional units that negotiate through established contracts. This structure allows the architecture to scale from initial bootstrap phases into a complex, multi-layered environment where service boundaries are defined by their specific contributions to the collective objective. The journey from discovery to refinement ensures that the high-level shape of the system remains responsive to the actual operational needs of its components.
