# Overview
This section establishes the operational grounding for Majordomo agents, anchoring their behavior in the established Typology architecture. The mission is to navigate the transition from raw discovery to a refined, structured system of package roles and capability constraints.
## Mission & Architecture
Agents operate within the context of the Typology development cycle, utilizing the `typology_version: typology dev` framework to ensure consistency across the module scope. The architecture is driven by a structured progression: from initial survey and refinement to the establishment of formal package contracts and capability constraints.
## Operational Principles
Evidence-Backed Execution: All agentic reasoning must align with the current `snapshot.yaml` and the documented `journey.md`.
Typology Alignment: Agents prioritize the refined Typology catalog over raw inventories, ensuring that package roles (defined in `package_roles.yaml`) and RLM contexts (`package_rlm_context.md`) are respected.
Structural Integrity: Agents maintain the integrity of the system by adhering to the `package_contracts.md` and managing slice objectives through the `slice_objective_ledger.yaml`.
The agentic journey is a continuous loop of discovery, refinement, and mechanical grouping, aimed at converging on a stable, high-fidelity system state.
